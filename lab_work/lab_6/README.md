# Lab 6 - Router Networks

Practical work on routing between IP subnets and VLANs in Cisco Packet Tracer.

Completed tasks:

- direct routing between two /24 networks on router R1;
- VLAN 10, 20, and 30 on switch SW2;
- 802.1Q trunk between SW2 and R2;
- router-on-a-stick on R2;
- connectivity tests with ping and tracert;
- shutdown and VLAN reassignment control tests;
- saving device configurations to startup-config.

Files:

- `lab_6.pdf` - completed worksheet/report;
- `lab_6.docx` - editable completed worksheet;
- `lab_6.pkt` - Cisco Packet Tracer project;
- `submission/` - device configurations and variant data.
